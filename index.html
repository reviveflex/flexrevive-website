About Flex Revive

Recovery should be purposeful.

Pain is only one part of the problem. Effective rehabilitation often means understanding movement, strength, mobility, coordination, balance, habits, workload, and your individual goals — not just treating a painful body part.

At Flex Revive, physiotherapy is structured around assessment, individualized treatment, active rehabilitation, education, and a progressive return to function.

Return to life

Back to work, sport and independence.

Meet Your Physiotherapist

Dr. Ashique Billah (PT)

Dr. Ashique Billah (PT) — Founder, Flex Revive.

Dr. Ashique Billah (PT) is a qualified physiotherapist with a Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT) from SSKM Hospital / IPGME&R, Kolkata.

His clinical exposure includes internship experience at SSKM Hospital and experience at Bangur Institute of Neurosciences. He has also been associated with Star Badminton Academy, providing exposure to sports-related rehabilitation and performance-oriented physiotherapy.

He currently practices in Kolkata and Baruipur and also provides teleconsultation to patients across India and internationally.
